# Risparmio - Your Personal Finance Companion

## Overview

Risparmio (Italian for saving) is a mobile application designed to help users manage their personal finances effectively and intuitively.

 The app is fully functional on real Android mobile phones, not just emulators. You can install it on an actual device and record videos of its functionality.

## Key Features

Expense Tracking:Easily record daily expenses with details such as:

Date

Category

Amount

Description

Take Picture Feature: Option to take a photo of receipts and store it along with the expense.

Budgeting and Goals:Set monthly minimum and maximum spending goals for different categories and track your progress.

Expense History:Review a detailed history of expenses with powerful filtering options:

Date range

Category

Search by description

Currency Flexibility:The app supports ZAR (South African Rand), with the potential to add more currencies in future versions.

Data Visualization:Interactive charts and graphs help you:

Track spending trends over time

Monitor adherence to budget goals

Visualize progress across different categories

Challenges & Badges (Custom Feature):A custom feature added to enhance user engagement:

Complete daily tasks related to expense tracking.

Earn badges for accomplishing financial challenges and maintaining consistency.

Example: Track expenses 5 days in a row → Unlock a badge!


## How Risparmio Works: A User Guide

Getting Started
Login:When you launch the app, you'll be prompted to log in with your username and password.If you do not have an account, tap Sign Up to create one.

Navigating the App
The app features an intuitive bottom navigation bar for easy access to the main sections:

Home:Overview of your current financial status:

Recent achievements

Spending summaries

Quick actions

Analyse:View interactive charts to analyze your spending by category and time period.Use the dropdown menu to select the time frame you wish to analyze.

Add:Quickly add new expenses.

History:View a detailed list of past expenses.Use:

Date filters

Category filters

Search bar (filter by description)

You can also log out from this screen using the Logout button.

Adding Expenses
Tap the "➕ Add" button in the bottom navigation bar.

Fill out the expense details:

Date: Select the expense date.

Category: Choose the appropriate category (e.g., Food, Transport, Entertainment).

Start Time / End Time: Optionally specify a time range.

Amount: Enter the amount spent (currency: Rand).

Description: Enter a short description of the expense.

Take Picture: Use the Take Picture button to capture a photo of the receipt (optional but recommended for record-keeping).

Additional Features

🏆 Daily Challenges & Badges (Custom Feature)

Earn points and unlock badges by completing predefined daily tasks, such as:

Tracking expenses for consecutive days

Staying within your budget goals

Completing weekly savings challenges

📊 Progress Charts

Spending Per Category:Visualize your spending per category over a user-selectable period.

Budget Goal Tracking:View how well you are staying between your minimum and maximum spending goals over the past month.

Progress Over Time:See visual feedback on budget adherence trends.

Compatibility

Works perfectly on real Android smartphones. The app can be installed via APK on a device and used in the real world (tested and confirmed). Suitable for demo recordings on actual phones.
